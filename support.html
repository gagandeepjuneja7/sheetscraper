Activate Pro

  1. Find the license key in your purchase email from Dodo Payments.
  2. Open the SheetScraper side panel in Chrome and click Upgrade Pro.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate.

One key works on up to 5 browsers. To move it, click PRO ACTIVE in the old browser and deactivate the license there first.

A page will not scan

  • When Chrome asks for permission to read the site, click Allow. If you clicked Deny, click Auto-Detect again.
  • Chrome does not let extensions read chrome:// pages or the Chrome Web Store.
  • If Auto-Detect misses the data, try Point & Click and click one of the items you want.

Refunds and cancelling

Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our Merchant of Record. To cancel the monthly plan or ask for a refund, email us with your purchase email address. After a refund the license key is switched off.
