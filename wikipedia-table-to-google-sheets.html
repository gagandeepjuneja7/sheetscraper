Wikipedia table to Google Sheets: IMPORTHTML and a one-click option

IMPORTHTML is the standard answer, and it works until it does not. Here is the exact formula, why it sometimes fails, and a faster route when you just want the table in your sheet.

The free way: the IMPORTHTML formula

Google Sheets has a built-in function that reads tables from a web page. Click an empty cell (A1 is fine) and type:

=IMPORTHTML("https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_largest_cities", "table", 1)

The three parts are the page address, the word "table" (use "list" for bullet lists) and the position of the table on the page. Press Enter and the table fills in below and to the right. If you see the wrong table, change 1 to 2, 3 and so on until the right one appears.

Two tips. Keep the cells below and to the right of your formula empty, or Sheets shows a #REF! error because it has no room to fill the result. And if you want a fixed copy that never changes, select the result, copy it, then use Edit > Paste special > Values only.

Where IMPORTHTML falls short:

  • You have to guess the table number. On a long article you may try several before you find yours.
  • Footnote markers such as [1] can appear inside cells, and merged cells can shift values, so some cleanup is normal.
  • It returns #N/A or "imported content is empty" on pages that build their table with JavaScript, need a login, or block automated requests.
  • The page is fetched by Google's servers, not by your browser, so it cannot see a page that only works when you are signed in.

Questions

Why does IMPORTHTML say "imported content is empty"?

The page may build its table with JavaScript, may need a login, may block automated requests, or the table number you gave does not exist. Try a different number first. If it still fails, copy the table from your open tab with a tool that reads the page as you see it.

How do I stop the data from changing?

Select the imported range, copy it, then use Edit > Paste special > Values only. The cells become plain values that no longer follow the page.

Can IMPORTHTML import a list instead of a table?

Yes. Use "list" instead of "table" as the second argument and give the list number.
