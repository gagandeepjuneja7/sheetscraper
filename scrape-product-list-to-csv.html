Scrape a product list or catalogue to CSV without code

Most shops show products as cards, not tables. Point & Click turns every card on the page into a row: click one, get them all.

How to do it, step by step

  1. Install SheetScraper and open the catalogue or search results page.
  2. Click the SheetScraper icon, then click Point & Click.
  3. Hover one product card. SheetScraper outlines it so you can see what it will pick.
  4. Click the card once. SheetScraper finds every card with the same structure and adds each one as a row, with its text, product link and image link.
  5. Export. Click CSV or JSON, or Copy to Sheets. For page 2, click Next Page, then scan again.

Tips for clean product data

Try Auto-Detect first. On many shop pages it finds the product grid without any clicks. Use Point & Click when it picks the wrong block.

Scroll before you scan. If the shop loads more products as you scroll, scroll to the end first so they are on the page.

Respect the site. Only collect data you are allowed to collect, and follow the site's terms.

Questions

Which columns do I get?

Each card becomes one row with the card's text, the product link and the image link. Open the column editor to rename, hide or reorder columns, trim spaces and remove duplicates.

Can it scrape all pages of a catalogue?

Yes. Click Collect pages and SheetScraper clicks Next or scrolls for you, up to 50 pages, and merges them into one table without duplicate rows. You can still click Next Page and scan one page at a time.

How many products can I export for free?

Up to 50 rows per scan on the free plan. Pro is unlimited: $30 once or $9 per month, taxes included.
